Pavimento Fertile is an artistic collective that brings together dancers, DJs, producers and street artists.

We focus on events, shows and music productions, dance courses and workshops, DJ sets.

OUR VISION

Music and Dance are creative tools dedicated to personal expression and to the sharing of values and emotions. In a world where the focus of these disciplines is increasingly directed towards competition, the vision of Pavimento Fertile remains focused on artistic and performative freedom.
