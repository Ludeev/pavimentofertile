Dancing Around Fire

Is the result of the collaboration between Tommaso Cappellato, drummer and producer based in Los Angeles, and Paolo "Frigo" Montesi, founder of Pavimento Fertile. The album combines elements of dance and music as a means of expressing ancestral rituals of freedom.


The music on the album is a fusion of breakbeats and experimental music, recorded by Tommaso Cappellato in Los Angeles and featuring singer Lalin St. Juste (of The Seshen), King Pari founders DJ Stepmom and Cameron Kinghorn, NY-based saxophonist and former Lounge Lizards band member Michael Blake, and Italian bass player Andrea Lombardini. Mixed by Garfield Adams (mixing engineer for Shafiq Husayn's The Loop) and mastered by Kelly Hibbert (who mastered many of Madlib and J Dilla's releases).
