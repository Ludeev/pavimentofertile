Pavimento Fertile Presents:

COSMIC HEALING MANTRAS

Hip Hop Dance, Live Painting, Musical Improvisation Show.

Concept:
Music, Dance and Painting are our Mantras. The Practice of Hip Hop arts becomes a daily ritual to find well-being, care, refuge. The repetition of sounds, movements and techniques becomes a meditative practice, a process of research towards inner depth and at the same time towards cosmic immensity. A dimension made of energy and emotions.

The result of these artistic explorations gives shape to a show that intersects choreographies and solos of the various Hip Hop dances (Breaking / Locking / Hip Hop / House), Live Painting performances and original music produced with drum machines, samplers and synths overlapped with quotes from vinyl records.

With:
Paolo "Frigo" Montesi
Davide "Collo" Del Gaia
Roberto "Rob Mystic" Del Gaia
Renato "Buzz" Scapecchi

Duration: 30 minutes.
