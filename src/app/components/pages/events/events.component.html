Pavimento Fertile Jam Session

Pavimento Fertile events are born from the need to offer a creative space, where you can build, share and enjoy artistic experiences. Our “Jam Session” is a moment of encounter and exchange between Dancers, Musicians, DJs and Street Artists.

Since 2010 we have realized over 20 editions in different locations: dance centers, discotheques, public squares, clubs. Over the years the event has grown to host internationally recognized dancers and music artists from Europe and the United States, attracting an ever-increasing number of enthusiasts and participants.
